Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

MittentisAi is a free, open-source project run by Kannan Murugapandian. It is a read-only website: it has no accounts, forms, comments or other ways for visitors to submit information. Contact: kannan.mp@proton.me.

1. What we collect

We do not ask for, store or process personal information from visitors, and the application keeps no visitor database. The only data the application stores is the generated stories, one featured image, and a list of past topics, none of which relates to a person.

2. Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on PythonAnywhere. Like most web hosts, PythonAnywhere may automatically record technical data such as your IP address, the time of the request, the page requested and your browser type in server logs. These logs are managed by PythonAnywhere under its own privacy policy, and we do not use them to identify or track you. Servers may be located outside your country, so this data may be transferred internationally.

3. Cookies and third parties

The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Pages load no fonts, scripts, images or other files from third-party servers. Links to other sites, such as GitHub, only send data to those sites if you click them.

4. How the content is made

Stories and images are generated automatically using Google's Gemini API. The prompts are written by the application and contain only generic instructions such as a genre; they contain no visitor data. We ask Google not to store these requests (the store=false option), but Google processes them under its own Gemini API terms, which may allow content sent on the free tier to be used to improve its products. The same server also generates an educational audio podcast that is published through Podbean. Podbean is a separate service with its own privacy policy that applies if you use it.

5. Legal basis and retention

Where technical data is processed to run and secure the site, we rely on our legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). The application itself retains no visitor data. Retention of logs is set by PythonAnywhere.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Singapore's PDPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you, to object to its processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because the application holds no visitor data, there is usually nothing for us to retrieve, but you can email us and we will help where we reasonably can, including by pointing you to PythonAnywhere.

7. Children

The site collects no information from anyone, including children. Some stories, such as horror or dystopian fiction, may contain mature themes, so parents and guardians should use discretion.

8. AI disclosure

All stories and illustrations are generated by AI and are labelled as such. They are published without human review and may be inaccurate or unsuitable.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
